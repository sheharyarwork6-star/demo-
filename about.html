The House

About CERIS

CERIS was created with a simple belief: skincare should feel effortless.

Our Story

A quieter way to care for skin.

Our goal is to create products that fit naturally into everyday routines while maintaining a sense of quality, simplicity, and modern beauty.

CERIS began as an edit. Too many formulas, too many promises, too much noise. We wanted a house that could stand beside the world’s most considered beauty brands — not by adding more, but by choosing better.

Our Philosophy

Less, but better.

We believe luxury is a feeling of ease. A cleanser that never tightens. A cream that hydrates without weight. Packaging that looks as refined on a marble vanity as it feels in the hand.

The CERIS philosophy is editorial: every ingredient, every texture, every line of copy is considered. If it does not serve the skin — or the experience — it does not belong.

Our Approach

Formulas with intention.

We work with a short list of well-understood ingredients — marshmallow root, aloe, glycerin, niacinamide, vitamin C derivatives, hyaluronic acid — and we give them space to perform.

The collection is designed to be complete at two products, and ready to grow without losing its clarity. Future formulas will follow the same rule: essential, elegant, and made for daily life.

Our Vision

Modern beauty, globally felt.

CERIS by Gamians aspires to be a quiet international house — a brand found on considered vanities from London to Seoul, recognized not by volume but by restraint.

We are building the architecture now: a focused collection, a cart ready for commerce, and an atelier prepared for the formulas still to come.
