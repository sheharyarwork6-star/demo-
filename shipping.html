Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Orders are prepared in the CERIS studio and typically dispatched within 2–4 business days. A flat delivery contribution of $12 applies. Complimentary shipping is included on orders of $90 or more.

Returns

If an unused product does not feel right, you may return it within 14 days of delivery. Please write to hello@cerisbygamians.com with your order reference so we can arrange a considered return.

Opened skincare cannot be restocked for hygiene reasons, unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.
